What happened

The Special Inspector General for Afghanistan Reconstruction issued its final report in December 2025, after two decades of oversight.

It found $26 billion to $29 billion of more than $148 billion in reconstruction spending was lost to waste, fraud and abuse.

Why nobody caught it

  • Much of the money moved through contractors in a war zone, far from the people checking the work.
