The data

Everything on this site comes from public records. Here it is in files you can open in a spreadsheet.

FileSizeWhat's in it
improper-payments-by-program.csv1,705 rowsEvery program's result for every year, FY2004–FY2025, from the government's own file. Money in millions of dollars; rate is improper plus unverifiable payments ÷ outlays.
improper-payments-by-year.csv22 rowsAll programs combined, one row per fiscal year (our sums).
cases.json43 rowsEvery case on this site with its receipt lines, evidence level and full source list.
cases.csv43 rowsThe same cases, one row each, for spreadsheets.
contract-obligations-by-month.csv108 rowsFederal contract obligations by fiscal month (October = 1), FY2018–FY2026, from USAspending.gov.
sources.csv131 rowsEvery source this site cites.

How to cite it

FraudBad, "Improper payments by program, FY2004–FY2025," fraudbad.com/data, updated November 8, 2026. Based on PaymentAccuracy.gov's FY2025 dataset.

Use it

Government figures are public domain. Our compilation is free to use with credit (CC BY 4.0). Charts and receipts on this site can be embedded: every case page has an embed code.

Want to stop it?Sign the Receipts Act.

Public money should come with a receipt anyone can check. The RECEIPTS Act starts with one rule for federal purchases over $25,000 from GSA's buying catalog: publish the price paid next to what other agencies paid for the same thing.
