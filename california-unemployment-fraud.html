$20 billion+: Unemployment money California paid to fraudsters

California's unemployment agency told lawmakers at least $20 billion in pandemic benefits went to fraud.

What happened

In October 2021, the head of California's Employment Development Department told a legislative hearing that at least $20 billion in unemployment benefits had gone to fraud since the pandemic began. That was more than 11 percent of everything paid.

Why nobody caught it

  • Claims surged into the millions within weeks, and identity checks didn't keep up.
