$162 billion in one month: The September rush: spend it before it expires

Agencies signed $161.9B in contracts in September 2025 — 19.5% of the year's contract money in the last month, 2.7 times an average month.

What happened

The federal fiscal year ends September 30, and one-year money that isn't committed by then can no longer be used for new contracts. We pulled every month of federal contract obligations from USAspending.gov for fiscal 2018 through 2025.

September was the biggest month in all 8 years. In fiscal 2025 agencies obligated $161.9B in September — 19.5% of the year — against an average of $60.6B in each of the other eleven months.

Why nobody caught it

  • Economists Jeffrey Liebman and Neale Mahoney found agencies spend 4.9 times a normal week's amount in the last week of the year, and that last-week IT projects were 2 to 6 times more likely to be rated lower quality.
  • USAspending shows when a contract was signed, not why. Nothing in the public record explains the timing.
