Answers / What did state auditors find in federal single audits?

Across all 50 states and DC, 43 states' latest federal single audits put a dollar figure on questioned costs where spending broke rules or lacked records.

Any state spending $1 million or more in federal funds must file an independent single audit with the Federal Audit Clearinghouse. Highlighted large-state findings from our 51-state review:

Public money should come with a receipt anyone can check. The RECEIPTS Act starts with one rule for federal purchases over $25,000 from GSA's buying catalog: publish the price paid next to what other agencies paid for the same thing.
