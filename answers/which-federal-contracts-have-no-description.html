Which major federal contracts have no public description?

Answers / Which major federal contracts have no public description?

Among the 100 largest federal contracts active in FY2026 on USAspending.gov, 23 totaling $285.4B have blank descriptions, filing codes only, or three words or fewer.

FraudBad pulled the 100 largest active FY2026 contract awards from USAspending.gov: 9 had blank descriptions or only filing codes, and 14 more used three words or fewer. A missing description is not proof of waste — it means taxpayers cannot see what was purchased.

Public money should come with a receipt anyone can check. The RECEIPTS Act starts with one rule for federal purchases over $25,000 from GSA's buying catalog: publish the price paid next to what other agencies paid for the same thing.
