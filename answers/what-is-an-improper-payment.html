What is an improper payment?

Answers / What is an improper payment?

An improper payment is federal money paid in the wrong amount, to the wrong person, or without the records to prove it was right. Agencies reported $185.8B of them in fiscal 2025.

The Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019 requires agencies to measure these payments every year, publish them and cut them. Payments the agency can't prove right or wrong are counted too, as "unknown" payments.

Most improper payments aren't fraud. By the government's own root-cause count, $123.0B of fiscal 2025's total went out wrong even though the information to stop it existed and the agency could get to it. More on that.

America.co · The RECEIPTS Act

Want to stop it?Sign the Receipts Act.

Public money should come with a receipt anyone can check. The RECEIPTS Act starts with one rule for federal purchases over $25,000 from GSA's buying catalog: publish the price paid next to what other agencies paid for the same thing.
