How much improper payment money does the government recover?

Answers / How much improper payment money does the government recover?

In fiscal 2025, federal agencies recovered $47.8B after flagging $77.5B in overpayments to claw back. Since FY2016, agencies recovered $447.0B of $897.4B identified.

OMB's FY2025 dataset tracks overpayments agencies identified for recapture and the amounts actually recovered each fiscal year (recoveries in a year can include overpayments flagged in earlier years):
