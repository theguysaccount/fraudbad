Answers

Straight answers

The questions people ask most, each answered with one sourced number first.

  • How much fraud is there in the federal government?The Government Accountability Office estimates the federal government loses $233 billion to $521 billion a year to fraud.
  • What is an improper payment?An improper payment is federal money paid in the wrong amount, to the wrong person, or without the records to prove it was right. Agencies reported $185.8B of them in fiscal 2025.
  • How much money has the federal government paid wrong?Federal agencies have reported $2.98 trillion in improper and unverifiable payments since fiscal 2004, by our sum of the government's own data.
  • Which federal programs have the most improper payments?Medicaid had the most in fiscal 2025: $37.4B paid wrong or without proof. The ten biggest programs made up 82% of the $185.8B total (our math).
  • How much pandemic relief money was lost to fraud?Estimates run to hundreds of billions of dollars. At least 17% of all Paycheck Protection Program and Covid disaster-loan money went out on potentially fraudulent applications.
  • Which states have the highest SNAP error rates?Alaska had the highest SNAP payment error rate in fiscal 2025, at 23.15%. The national rate was 10.62%.

Want to stop it?Sign the Receipts Act.

Public money should come with a receipt anyone can check. The RECEIPTS Act starts with one rule for federal purchases over $25,000 from GSA's buying catalog: publish the price paid next to what other agencies paid for the same thing.
