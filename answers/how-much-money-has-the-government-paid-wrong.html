How much money has the federal government paid wrong?

Answers / How much money has the federal government paid wrong?

Federal agencies have reported $2.98 trillion in improper and unverifiable payments since fiscal 2004, by our sum of the government's own data.

Through fiscal 2024 the total is $2.80 trillion, which matches GAO's published figure of about $2.8 trillion. Fiscal 2025 alone added $185.8B.

The total only counts programs that report. Some large programs, such as Temporary Assistance for Needy Families, have never been included.

Public money should come with a receipt anyone can check. The RECEIPTS Act starts with one rule for federal purchases over $25,000 from GSA's buying catalog: publish the price paid next to what other agencies paid for the same thing.
