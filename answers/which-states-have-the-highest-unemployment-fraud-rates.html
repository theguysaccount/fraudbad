Which states have the highest unemployment improper payment rates?

Answers / Which states have the highest unemployment improper payment rates?

Kentucky had the highest unemployment improper-payment rate in the 12 months ending March 31, 2026, at 38.03% (national rate: 12.11%).

The U.S. Department of Labor checks a random sample of every state's unemployment benefits each year through its Benefit Accuracy Measurement scorecard. The federal compliance target is under 10%.
