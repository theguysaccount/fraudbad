Which federal agencies failed payment integrity compliance?

Answers / Which federal agencies failed payment integrity compliance?

15 of 63 rated federal agencies were found non-compliant with the Payment Integrity Information Act (PIIA) for fiscal 2024.

Inspectors general audit each agency's payment-integrity reporting every year under the Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019. These agencies were rated non-compliant in OMB's FY2025 dataset (FY2024 compliance column):
