Answers / How much pandemic relief money was lost to fraud?

Estimates run to hundreds of billions of dollars. At least 17% of all Paycheck Protection Program and Covid disaster-loan money went out on potentially fraudulent applications.

No single official total covers every pandemic program. These are the biggest estimates, each from the agency or watchdog that made it:

America.co · The RECEIPTS Act

Want to stop it?Sign the Receipts Act.

Public money should come with a receipt anyone can check. The RECEIPTS Act starts with one rule for federal purchases over $25,000 from GSA's buying catalog: publish the price paid next to what other agencies paid for the same thing.
