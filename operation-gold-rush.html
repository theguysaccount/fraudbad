$10.6 billion billed: Fake catheter claims using a million stolen identities

Prosecutors say one network billed Medicare $10.6 billion for medical supplies nobody needed or received.

What happened

Prosecutors say a transnational ring bought medical supply companies and used them to bill Medicare for urinary catheters and other equipment. They used the stolen identities of more than a million Americans.

It was the largest scheme in the Justice Department's June 2025 health care fraud takedown, which charged 324 people over $14.6 billion in alleged fraud.

Why nobody caught it

  • Each claim used a real person's Medicare number, so one at a time they looked routine.
  • Medicare stopped most of the money. Supplemental insurers still paid out about $900 million.
