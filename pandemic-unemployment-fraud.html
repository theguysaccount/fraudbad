$100–135 billion: Stolen from pandemic unemployment benefits

Between 11 and 15 cents of every pandemic unemployment dollar went to fraud, GAO estimates.

What happened

When Covid shut down the economy, Congress expanded unemployment benefits fast and states rushed to pay claims. Identity thieves filed in state after state using stolen personal information.

In September 2023 GAO estimated that $100 billion to $135 billion of pandemic unemployment money went to fraud.

Why nobody caught it

  • States were under pressure to pay quickly, and new federal programs let people certify their own eligibility.
  • At the time of the estimate, states had identified only $5.3 billion in fraud and recovered $1.2 billion.

How sure are we?
