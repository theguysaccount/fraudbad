What happened

Feeding Our Future, a Minnesota nonprofit, sponsored sites that claimed to serve meals to children during the pandemic using federal child nutrition money.

Prosecutors said the sites billed for meals that were never served. A jury convicted founder Aimee Bock, and in May 2026 she was sentenced to 500 months in prison and ordered to repay nearly $243 million.

Why nobody caught it

  • Reimbursement was based on meal counts the sites reported themselves.
