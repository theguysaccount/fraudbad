$77 million: Welfare money for the poorest, steered elsewhere

Mississippi's welfare scandal ran through TANF, the one big program left out of the federal government's improper-payment total.

What happened

Federal Temporary Assistance for Needy Families money meant for Mississippi's poorest families was steered to people and projects with no connection to helping them, including sports figures.

Seven people have pleaded guilty, including the former director of the Mississippi Department of Human Services. The first sentencing came in May 2026.

Why nobody caught it

  • TANF gives states wide room to decide how to spend. GAO names it as a program left out of the government-wide improper-payment estimate.
