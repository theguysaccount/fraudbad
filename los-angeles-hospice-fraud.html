$600 million: Los Angeles hospices cut off over suspected fraud

Federal officials froze Medicare payments to hundreds of Los Angeles hospices and home health agencies in 2026.

What happened

In April 2026 federal officials suspended Medicare payments to 447 hospices and 23 home health agencies in Southern California over about $600 million in suspected fraud. By May, CMS said it had suspended about 800 hospices and home health agencies in Los Angeles.

On May 13, 2026, CMS froze new Medicare enrollment for hospices and home health agencies nationwide for six months.

Why nobody caught it

  • Hospices were enrolled first and checked later. CMS said its existing tools mostly act after enrollment.

How sure are we?

Payments frozen

Officials stopped payment over suspected fraud. No one has been found guilty.

Payment suspensions are not convictions. They mean officials believe there is credible evidence of fraud.
