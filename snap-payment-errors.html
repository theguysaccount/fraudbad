$10.1 billion: Food aid paid in the wrong amount

More than one in ten SNAP dollars went out wrong in fiscal 2025.

What happened

USDA audits a sample of SNAP cases in every state each year. For fiscal 2025 it found 10.62 percent of benefits were paid in the wrong amount, $10.1 billion counting both overpayments and underpayments.

Why nobody caught it

  • States decide who qualifies and how much they get. The federal spending database can't trace SNAP money below the state level.

How sure are we?

Paid without proof

Officially counted as paid in error or without the records to prove it was right. Some is fraud. Nobody has measured how much.

The error rate counts mistakes as well as fraud, and it includes people who were underpaid.
