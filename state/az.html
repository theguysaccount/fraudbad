Arizona

In fiscal 2025, Arizona got 10.80% of its food-benefit (SNAP) payments wrong — #19 of 51, where #1 is worst. The national rate was 10.62%.

What Arizona's auditors found

$13.6MEducation Department purchasing · counted by the auditors

The Arizona Department of Education's procurement division bought $30.2 million of goods and services without following state procurement law, $13.6 million of it in fiscal 2024. The state reported it to the auditors and the attorney general as potential fraud.

Arizona Auditor General: State of Arizona single audit, fiscal year ended June 30, 2024, finding 2024-111. Filed Apr 27, 2026. How we picked this.

Also: $133M–$493M missed in potential cost reductions in fiscal 2026 because Arizona's Medicaid agency didn't use the standardized assessment a 2025 state law required for its program paying parents as caregivers. Special Audit of the Arizona Parents as Paid Caregivers Service Delivery Model (26-105), Jul 2026
