Unemployment benefits paid wrong

21.01%of unemployment benefits overpaid or underpaid · #7 of 51, #1 worst

In the 12 months ending March 31, 2026, Michigan paid 21.01% of its unemployment benefits in the wrong amount, up from 20.68% a year earlier. The national rate was 12.11%. The federal goal is under 10%; Michigan is above it.

The same sample found 2.85% of benefits went out on payments the Labor Department counts as fraud (national: 4.00%).

The Labor Department checks a random sample of each state's payments every year (its Benefit Accuracy Measurement program). Like the SNAP rate, "paid wrong" counts paying too much and too little.
