Vermont

In fiscal 2025, Vermont got 5.38% of its food-benefit (SNAP) payments wrong — #46 of 51, where #1 is worst. The national rate was 10.62%.

Unemployment benefits paid wrong

7.41%of unemployment benefits overpaid or underpaid · #40 of 51, #1 worst

In the 12 months ending March 31, 2026, Vermont paid 7.41% of its unemployment benefits in the wrong amount, down from 8.13% a year earlier. The national rate was 12.11%. The federal goal is under 10%; Vermont is under it.

The same sample found 0.89% of benefits went out on payments the Labor Department counts as fraud (national: 4.00%).
