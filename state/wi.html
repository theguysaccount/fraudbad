Wisconsin

In fiscal 2025, Wisconsin got 5.72% of its food-benefit (SNAP) payments wrong — #44 of 51, where #1 is worst. The national rate was 10.62%.

Unemployment benefits paid wrong

19.71%of unemployment benefits overpaid or underpaid · #9 of 51, #1 worst

In the 12 months ending March 31, 2026, Wisconsin paid 19.71% of its unemployment benefits in the wrong amount, down from 22.66% a year earlier. The national rate was 12.11%. The federal goal is under 10%; Wisconsin is above it.

The same sample found 2.21% of benefits went out on payments the Labor Department counts as fraud (national: 4.00%).

The Labor Department checks a random sample of each state's payments every year (its Benefit Accuracy Measurement program). Like the SNAP rate, "paid wrong" counts paying too much and too little.
