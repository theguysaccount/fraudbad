What happened

The Paycheck Protection Program and Covid Economic Injury Disaster Loans moved about $1.2 trillion in a matter of months.

In June 2023 the Small Business Administration's inspector general estimated more than $200 billion of it went out on applications that showed signs of fraud.

Why nobody caught it

  • Applicants largely vouched for themselves, and money moved in days instead of weeks.
  • Nearly $30 billion has been seized or returned. That is less than a sixth of the estimate.

How sure are we?

Estimated

An auditor, inspector general or the agency itself estimated the fraud. It's a measured range, not a list of names.

"Potentially fraudulent" means the application tripped fraud warning signs. It is an inspector general's estimate, not a count of convictions.
