What happened

In December 2025 the Defense Department failed its eighth straight financial audit. It is the only one of the government's 24 major agencies that has never passed.

Auditors flagged 26 material weaknesses. In one example, the department couldn't produce reliable data to verify the global pool of F-35 spare parts.

Why nobody caught it

  • When auditors can't verify the books, nobody can rule theft or waste in or out.

How sure are we?

Can't be checked

The books or the spending can't be verified, so fraud can't be ruled in or out.

A failed audit is not proof of fraud. It means the records can't prove where the money and equipment are.
