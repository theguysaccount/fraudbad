The ledger

Every year, federal agencies have to measure how much they paid in the wrong amount, to the wrong person, or without the paperwork to prove it was right. They report it to the White House budget office, which publishes one spreadsheet. We read every row. Here it is.

The law

15 of 63 agencies broke it

The Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019 requires agencies to measure improper payments, publish them and cut them. Inspectors general check every year. For fiscal 2024, these agencies were rated non-compliant: AmeriCorps, Labor, Defense (War), Election Assistance Commission, EPA, FCC, Health and Human Services, Housing and Urban Development, Personnel Management, Small Business Administration, Social Security, Selective Service, Treasury, Agriculture, Veterans Affairs.

Source: FY2025 dataset, "Compliance Trend Table," fiscal 2024 column. Fiscal 2025 ratings come out in 2026's dataset.
