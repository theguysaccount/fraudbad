$28.1 billion: Refundable tax credits paid out in error

About one in four dollars of the IRS's refundable tax credits went out improperly in fiscal 2025.

What happened

The Treasury Department's tax watchdog reported in July 2026 that improper payments on the IRS's refundable tax credits rose to about $28.1 billion in fiscal 2025.

The error rate climbed from 21.9 percent to 26.5 percent in one year. GAO lists the Earned Income Tax Credit among the five programs holding 73 percent of all federal improper payments.

Why nobody caught it

  • Eligibility rules are complicated, and much of what decides eligibility can't be checked before the refund goes out.

How sure are we?

Paid without proof

Officially counted as paid in error or without the records to prove it was right. Some is fraud. Nobody has measured how much.

Improper payments include honest mistakes. Nobody has split out how much is fraud.
