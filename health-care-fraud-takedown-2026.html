What happened

On June 23, 2026 the Justice Department announced the largest health care fraud takedown by number of defendants: 455 people across 56 federal districts and 45 states and territories.

The cases range from medically unnecessary tests to wound-care products billed to Medicare and Medicaid.

Why nobody caught it

  • These cases were built after the claims were filed. The announcement does not say how much was paid out before anyone caught it.
