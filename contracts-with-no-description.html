What happened

We pulled the 100 largest federal contracts that had spending activity in fiscal 2026 from USAspending.gov, the government's own spending site. Together they're worth $1,171.8B.

9 of them, worth $160.4B, show either a blank description or only an internal code like "IGF::OT::IGF." Another 14, worth $125.1B, say three words or fewer.

Why nobody caught it

  • Agencies enter these descriptions themselves. No rule holds up a payment when the field is blank.
  • The full list, with links to every award, is on the Soft Fraud page.

How sure are we?

Can't be checked

The books or the spending can't be verified, so fraud can't be ruled in or out.

Measured by FraudBad from USAspending.gov on November 9, 2026. A blank description is not proof of waste; it means the public can't tell what was bought. Award values are lifetime totals for awards with activity in fiscal 2026.
