$8.5 billion: Fraudulent jobless claims Michigan likely paid

A consulting firm hired by the state estimated Michigan paid about $8.5 billion in fraudulent unemployment benefits in 19 months.

What happened

Deloitte, which Michigan hired to investigate, estimated about $8.5 billion in fraudulent jobless benefits went out during 19 months of the pandemic: $2.7 billion to $2.8 billion to impostors and $5.6 billion to $5.7 billion to people who misrepresented their eligibility.

Why nobody caught it

  • A separate state audit found $3.9 billion in payments to people later found ineligible.
