$4.35 billion HUD can't verify: Rent subsidies HUD can't verify

In fiscal 2025, HUD said it couldn't determine whether $4.35B of its project-based rental assistance — 26.4% of the program — was paid correctly. A year earlier the figure was $45 million.

What happened

HUD's project-based rental assistance pays private owners to keep apartments affordable for low-income tenants.

In its fiscal 2025 report HUD put $4.35B in the "unknown" column: payments it couldn't prove right or wrong because the documentation wasn't there. In fiscal 2024 the same column held $45 million.

Why nobody caught it

  • HUD was one of the agencies out of compliance with the federal payment-integrity law in fiscal 2024.

How sure are we?

Can't be checked

The books or the spending can't be verified, so fraud can't be ruled in or out.

HUD's own figures in the official FY2025 dataset. "Unknown" means insufficient documentation, not proven fraud. The jump from fiscal 2024 may partly reflect a change in how HUD tested payments; the dataset doesn't say.
