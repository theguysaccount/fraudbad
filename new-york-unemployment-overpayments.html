What happened

In November 2022, New York State Comptroller Thomas DiNapoli's audit found at least $11 billion in unemployment overpayments during the pandemic, some of it fraud.

Why nobody caught it

  • The comptroller said the exact figure was hard to pin down because the Labor Department was slow to hand over information.

How sure are we?

Paid without proof

Officially counted as paid in error or without the records to prove it was right. Some is fraud. Nobody has measured how much.
